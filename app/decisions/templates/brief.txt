11. LIMITATIONS
- Deterministic rules only: no language model, embedding or prompt produced any
  part of this brief.
- The band is a policy artefact, not a probability of churn.
- Business days follow a Monday-to-Friday rule with no holiday calendar.
- Money is stated per currency. No exchange rate is applied, so no cross-currency
  total exists.
- Document links are derived by exact identifier and exact name matching only. A
  document that concerns the customer without naming it is linked to no one.
- The escalation path lists open tickets by their status, while the signals
  decide whether a ticket is open by its resolution date as of the evaluation
  date.
- Quoted document text is escaped and capped at 500 characters; a longer quote
  is cut there and marked as truncated after its closing quote. Offsets index
  the document's citable text: its title, a newline, then its body.
- Approver identity is recorded, not authenticated, and nothing in this brief is
  executed.
